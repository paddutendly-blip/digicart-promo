Privacy Policy

DigiCart promotion website and WhatsApp messages · Last updated 22 November 2026

Who we are

This website and the WhatsApp messages about DigiCart are run by DigiFactory Labs, Bengaluru, India. DigiCart is our app that helps local shops take orders online.

Contact: digifactorylabs@gmail.com · Phone / WhatsApp +91 95353 88375

This page covers our promotion of DigiCart to shop owners. The DigiCart app has its own privacy policy, shown on its Google Play page.

What we collect

  • Shop details: shop name, mobile number, area, type of shop, and notes from our visits or calls. These come from the shop owner, from public sources such as shop signboards and maps, or from our team.
  • WhatsApp messages: the messages we send you through the WhatsApp Business Platform (run by Meta), whether they were delivered or read, and any reply you send us.
  • Use of our pages: when you open a link we sent, we note which page and video you viewed, the buttons you tapped, roughly how long you stayed, the language you chose and your phone model. We do not use advertising cookies.

Why we use it

  • To tell shop owners about DigiCart and show them a demo of their shop online.
  • To follow up with shops that are interested, and to answer replies and questions.
  • To stop messaging shops that are not interested or ask us to stop.
  • To see which messages and videos work, so we can improve them.

Who else handles it

We do not sell your data. We use these services to run the promotion, and they process data for us:

  • Meta (WhatsApp Business Platform): sends our WhatsApp messages and tells us whether they were delivered.
  • Google (Sheets, Drive, Apps Script): where we keep shop details and message records.
  • GitHub Pages: hosts this website.

We may share information if the law requires it.

Stop messages

Reply STOP to any of our WhatsApp messages, or tap the STOP button. We mark your shop as not interested and send no more promotional messages. You can also call or WhatsApp us at +91 95353 88375.

Delete your data

To have your details deleted, send an email to digifactorylabs@gmail.com or a WhatsApp message to +91 95353 88375 with the subject Delete my data and the mobile number we contacted. We delete your shop details, message records and replies within 30 days and confirm by reply. Copies held by Meta under its own policies are handled by Meta.

How long we keep it and how we protect it

We keep shop details and message records only while they are useful for promoting DigiCart and following up, or until you ask us to delete them. Access is limited to our team and protected by Google account security and a passcode.

Changes

If we change this policy, we update this page and the date at the top.
